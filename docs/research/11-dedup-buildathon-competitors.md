# Pehchana Kaun? — competitor / dedup check, Bolna Symphony Buildathon

Checked 2026-10-21 (~22:00 IST). Read-only: gh CLI search/API, curl, WebSearch. No logins, no Slack, no posting.

## Verdict

**No high or medium match found.** Among the 16 repos whose README says they were built for the Bolna Symphony Buildathon (plus 2 empty buildathon-named repos and the Bolna-wall entries), and the 163 GitHub repos created since 2026-10-01 that mention "bolna", none is a human-vs-AI guessing game, a phone Turing test, a voice realism arena, or a TTS human-likeness leaderboard.

What our idea does run into is **prior art outside the buildathon**, and judges may bring it up:
1. **Kalpa Labs (a buildathon sponsor) came up with "Turing Clock" themselves.** In a blog post dated 2026-07-30 they define it as "how long can a person talk with a model before deducing, reliably better than 50% accuracy, that it's an AI? We expect to report this number for all of our new releases." https://kalpalabs.ai/blog/towards-generalist-audio-models . I found no public Turing Clock number, tool or leaderboard from them yet (homepage checked 2026-10-21: https://kalpalabs.ai/). [INFERRED] That helps more than it hurts: we would be the first public measurement tool for a metric a sponsor proposed. It also means we must credit Kalpa for the term and not claim we invented it.
2. **AudioRealismBench by Design Arena** already ranks TTS models by asking listeners "which clip sounds more human", with a real human recording as an anchor. Kalpa's homepage shows its Elo, "updated 11 Oct 2026" (Human recording 1476, Kalpa TTS Beta 1364, Cartesia Sonic 3.6 1339, Eleven v3 1232, GPT Realtime 2 1088, Eleven v4 1033). https://designarena.ai/leaderboard/audiorealismbench , https://www.designarena.ai/methodology/audio-realism-benchmark . So a **"TTS realism leaderboard" on its own is not new.** It is single-clip and pairwise. Ours measures multi-turn, live, on a phone call, with time-to-detection.
3. **Voice Arena (voicearena.com)** runs Indic TTS and STT leaderboards (Maya 2 Native, for example, is called "#1 Hindi streaming voice model on Voice Arena": https://x.com/Dheemanthredy/status/2085335847185048040). Same caveat as above: a TTS-comparison leaderboard already exists for Indic languages.
4. **Text Turing games** are well known: Human or Not (humanornot.so, 1.5M+ users, AI21 paper https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.20010), turingtest.live, playturing.com, play.turinggame.ai. I found none that runs on a **real phone line with voice agents in Indian languages.** [INFERRED] The new parts are phone, voice and Indic languages, plus time-to-detection.
